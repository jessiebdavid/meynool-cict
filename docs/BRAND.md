# Meynool Brand Decision

- **Tamil brand:** மெய்நூல்
- **Roman brand:** Meynool
- **Formal descriptor:** Tamil Literary Knowledge and Scientific Evidence Intelligence Platform
- **Tagline:** From Ancient Texts to Evidence-Based Discovery.

## Intended interpretation

- **மெய் (mey):** truth, reality, or what is real.
- **நூல் (nool):** book, text, or treatise.
- **மெய்நூல் (Meynool):** a coined brand name intended to evoke truthful knowledge grounded in texts. Do not claim that this compound has one fixed dictionary definition without linguistic validation.

Before public launch, ask a fluent Tamil scholar to validate spelling, pronunciation and cultural connotations, and separately check trademark, domain and repository-name availability.
